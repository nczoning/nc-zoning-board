      NIGHT CORP // ZONING BOARD - ABOUT

      > QUERYING SYSTEM MANIFEST...

      > RESULT: PROJECT_OVERVIEW_v0.1

      The NC Zoning Board is an interactive map and coordinate registry for Cyberpunk 2077 location mods.

      Its purpose is to show where location mods are placed across Night City, helping mod authors explore the landscape and coordinate with adjacent projects.

      Originally envisioned by Kaoziun and developed by the Locations Hub community.

      NIGHT CORP // SUBMIT A LOCATION
      1. TAG YOUR MOD: add the NCZoning tag to your mod on Nexus Mods. That is all the tag does now: it puts your mod in the list below, with its title, short description and uploader ready to edit. It is optional, and a mod submitted by link is treated exactly the same.
      2. ACQUIRE COORDINATES: open CET in-game, run the command below in the console. Copy the output and paste the values below:
        local p,r = GetPlayer():GetWorldPosition(), GetPlayer():GetWorldOrientation():ToEulerAngles(); print(string.format("x=%.4f y=%.4f z=%.4f yaw=%.4f", p.x, p.y, p.z, r.yaw))
      3. CONFIGURE METADATA: fill out the form below
      4. SUBMIT FOR REVIEW: complete the check at the bottom of the form and send it
      5. A REVIEWER DECIDES: nothing reaches the map until a reviewer approves it
      Submissions are anonymous, so nothing is sent back to you. Watch the map for your pin, or leave a contact below if you are happy to be asked a question about it.
      Mods tagged NCZoning on Nexus appear here. Most tagged mods are already on the map, so an empty list is normal. A new tag can take about 10 minutes to reach the Nexus API and a few minutes more to reach this list, so if you have just added it, paste your mod's link instead.

Coordinates can be negative: include the minus sign if there is one (e.g. -1234.56). Night City sits within about ±4000 on X and Y and ±300 on Z; anything past ±8000, or past -1000 to 2000 on Z, is refused.
